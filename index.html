Who we are

We are a private family office based in Singapore

G3 Infiniti exists to serve one family. We hold no outside capital and seek no clients. Our work is deliberately quiet: preserving what has been built, and preparing it for the hands that follow.

From Singapore, we look after the family’s investments, governance and practical affairs — patiently, and with permanence as the measure of every decision.

Our approach

We believe permanence comes from patience, governance and alignment

Decisions are made deliberately, held long, and reviewed with care. Structure does the work that urgency cannot.
